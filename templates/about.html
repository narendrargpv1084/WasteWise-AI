About the project

WasteWise AI

WasteWise AI helps people sort household waste by classifying a photo into one of 12 categories and pairing that category with practical disposal guidance.

Why it exists

Incorrect sorting contaminates recycling streams and sends recoverable material to landfill. A fast image check can reduce guesswork at the bin, especially for look-alike items such as glass colors or mixed packaging.

Model architecture

The classifier is a MobileNetV2 transfer-learning model trained in Google Colab and saved as wastewise_best.keras. MobileNetV2 is a compact convolutional network designed for efficient image classification. This application loads the trained weights for inference only — it does not retrain, fine-tune, or recompile the model.

  • Input RGB image resized to 224×224
  • Output Softmax over 12 waste classes
  • Validation accuracy ~95% on 3,103 validation images
  • Preprocessing Rescaling(1./127.5, offset=-1) is inside the Keras graph

12 supported categories

  • Battery
  • Biological
  • Brown Glass
  • Cardboard
  • Clothes
  • Green Glass
  • Metal
  • Paper
  • Plastic
  • Shoes
  • Trash
  • White Glass

Classification workflow

  1. You upload a PNG, JPG, or WEBP image, or capture one from the camera.
  2. The server checks the file type and size, then stores it with a safe unique name.
  3. The image is converted to RGB, resized to 224×224, and batched. No extra pixel normalization is applied.
  4. The saved MobileNetV2 model produces a softmax distribution over the 12 labels in class_names.json.
  5. The predicted class is shown with disposal guidance. The uploaded file is removed after inference.
